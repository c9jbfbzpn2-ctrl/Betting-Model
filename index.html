Where the numbers come from. Every line and price is Kalshi's own. No sportsbook odds are used. The chance of each bet winning comes from team ratings built from past game results (updated every few hours). The page compares that chance to Kalshi's price after estimated fees.

Confidence (1 to 5 stars) is how likely the bet is to win according to the team data: very low is under 30%, low 30 to 45%, medium 45 to 60%, high 60 to 75%, very high 75% and up. A very high bet at 90 cents can still be a bad price, which is what the verdict is for.

Verdict. Take: the team data says the chance is at least 5 points better than Kalshi's price. Lean: 2 to 5 points better. Pass: the price is fair or worse. Check: the gap is over 15 points, which usually means the ratings are missing something like an injury, so look before betting. Unrated: no team data for these teams, so only Kalshi's price is shown.

Units scale with confidence for Take bets (half a unit at 1 star up to 3 units at 5 stars); Lean bets get half of that, with a half-unit minimum. Pass, Check and Unrated get none. The dollar amount uses your unit size.

Be careful. The ratings have not been shown to beat Kalshi's prices. Kalshi's market already knows about injuries, weather and news that the ratings do not. Use small stakes until the track record above has plenty of graded bets, and treat a lot of Take bets on one slate as a sign to be skeptical, not excited.
